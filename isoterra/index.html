How to play
Tap a unit to select it, then tap a highlighted tile to move, or an enemy to attack.

Tap your city to train units. Tap 🧪 Tech to research new units & bonuses.

Hills give defenders a combat bonus. Villages (🛖) grant bonus stars when reached.

Capture the enemy capital (👑) to win.

Victory

You have conquered the enemy capital.
